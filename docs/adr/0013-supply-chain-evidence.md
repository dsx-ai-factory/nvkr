## Context

NKD emits a per-artifact supply-chain evidence set: SBOM, external references for `acquired` artifacts, and
vulnerability results. The set is deterministic, bound to artifact identity, and folds into the evidence bundle
(ADR-05). One boundary governs all of it: NKD **produces** evidence and does not operate the fleet.

## Decision

### 1. SBOM identity: per-artifact + aggregate index

Emit a **deterministic CycloneDX SBOM per built artifact**, bound to that artifact's digest, plus an **aggregate
index** linking them. Serialization is deterministic — the serial number derives from the recipe/artifact
identity, with **no timestamps and no random UUIDs** — so documents are byte-identical across runs and validate
and ingest cleanly into stock scanners. Publisher-generated SBOM identity is also **mirror-invariant**: source,
toolchain, and prebuilt components use stable semantic identifiers plus their exact content pins. A repository
hostname, registry prefix, mirror key, or other relocatable acquisition locator cannot perturb the per-artifact
SBOM, its aggregate index, or ADR-05's publisher-evidence digest while the content and recipe identities are
unchanged.

This identity rule applies to evidence **generated by the publisher from its qualified producer report**. It does
not erase the location of separately acquired upstream evidence, which is an authenticated acquisition record
governed by §2: the transport locator is preserved so an auditor can retrieve or mirror the declared bytes, and a
separate digest authenticates those bytes. Moving that external reference changes the acquisition declaration
even when the verified payload digest is unchanged; relocating the publisher's build inputs does not change
locally generated evidence identity.

### 2. External evidence for acquired artifacts

A `prebuilt` (no-compile) component carries a **verified reference** to the upstream's external SBOM/provenance.
The reference is a canonical transport location (HTTPS, OCI, or a mirror key); the content digest is a separate
exact identity, and the URL need not embed it. The referenced bytes **must hash to the declared digest or fail
closed**. Where no upstream evidence exists, the component declares an explicit **`evidence: none`** — never a
silent absence. That is **distinct from a Tier-4 exclusion** (`fromSource:false` plus a reason). One owning
schema defines the reference, consumed by both the acquire lane and the SBOM emitter.

`evidence: none` is an honest statement about an acquisition. It is **not evidence and never satisfies an
evidence-required promotion gate**. If release policy permits a missing evidence class, the exception is an
explicit, authenticated `EvidenceWaiverReceipt` naming the exact artifact digest, missing evidence types, policy
digest, reason, approver identity, and expiry. The waiver is policy evidence; it does not masquerade as an SBOM
or provenance receipt.

### 3. Vulnerability-report determinism

A **shelled** Apache-2.0 scanner (grype/trivy — subprocess, never linked) produces a report naming the **scanned
artifact, the pinned scanner DB/policy version, and the recipe identity**, with **scan-time omitted**. The
DB/policy version is an **explicit pinned input**: a "deterministic" report only holds if the moving DB is
captured as a pin. A missing scanner **fails closed**, or is print-only under an explicit flag. A de-risking
spike covers this (below).

### 4. Advisory → affected-recipe boundary

Advisory generation is outside the current product scope. The unused advisory emitter and scanner-argv
prototype are removed. The retained vulnerability module validates subject-bound scanner observations consumed
by release gates; it does not implement scanner execution or a fleet index.

### 5. Typed qualified capabilities and authenticated evidence receipts

Within one process, evidence becomes gate input only through an **opaque qualified capability**. Its private
state can be created only after the module has read and recomputed the exact subject and evidence bytes and
applied the typed verification policy. The capability is non-serializable, cannot be constructed from a decoded
report or a boolean, and is consumed before that process exits. Locally generated per-artifact SBOM/provenance
receipts, verified external-evidence receipts, and qualified vulnerability reports are distinct ephemeral forms;
release policy accepts each only for its exact artifact, evidence type, schema, and policy binding.

Any evidence crossing a persistence, process, registry, or organizational boundary uses a canonical,
digest-addressed receipt:

```
EvidenceReceipt {
  schemaVersion
  receiptType              # sbom | provenance | vulnerability | external
  artifactDigest           # exact subject bytes
  evidenceDigest           # exact canonical evidence bytes
  evidenceSchema
  verificationPolicyDigest
  producerIdentity
  verdict                  # accepted | rejected
}
```

The boundary-crossing receipt is detached-signed and authenticated against the trust policy for its
`receiptType`. The verifier recomputes both subject and evidence digests before issuing or accepting it. A
same-process capability MUST NOT be serialized as a substitute for this receipt; if it must outlive or leave its
issuer process, it is promoted into the signed form first.

A path, transport URL, mutable tag, untyped boolean, decoded report, or caller assertion such as `--verified` is
neither form. A qualified capability or receipt for one artifact, evidence document, schema, or policy cannot
satisfy another. Component-level references are not implicitly reusable across produced artifacts: every receipt
names the exact artifact subject it covers. ADR-16 consumes only the typed qualified capabilities or the
authenticated receipt set; raw evidence files remain the auditable payloads those receipts reference.

### 6. Pipeline SBOM and acquisition scope

V1 emits deterministic CycloneDX documents per observed component artifact and an aggregate index through a
per-recipe SBOM node after terminal observation. Each subject is the runner-observed artifact digest; the index
also binds Recipe Digest. Resolved component declarations are frozen in the plan so the node needs no live
catalog. Adding those declarations or outputs changes canonical plan identity and requires regeneration.

SBOMs assert composition. They remain `static-unqualified` in dry-run, and participate in pipeline-attested
evidence only through the signed Run's subject-bound closure; generation alone is no signature or reproduction
proof. Acquired components retain `evidence: none` unless pinned upstream evidence is declared. V1 does not
verify upstream signatures/provenance on acquisition. Vulnerability scanning stays outside v1.
Curated/embedded aggregates use dependency edges from aggregate to members (ADR-10/22).

## Consequences

**Positive**
- Every built artifact has deterministic, digest-bound SBOM and (via ADR-09) provenance an auditor can recompute.
- Acquired artifacts are honestly marked — verified reference or `evidence: none` — preserving the
  from-source XOR prebuilt XOR exclusion trichotomy.
- Promotion distinguishes present evidence, absent evidence, and an explicitly authorized waiver without
  converting absence into success.
- Vulnerability results are reproducible when the DB/policy is pinned, and consumable by stock scanners.

**Negative / costs**
- Vuln-report determinism requires provisioning and mirroring a pinned scanner DB/policy — an infra dependency
  for air-gap.
- External-evidence conventions vary per upstream; some references degrade to `evidence: none`.
- Persisted and cross-boundary receipt issuers and waiver approvers require explicit trust-policy roles and
  key-rotation procedures; same-process capabilities require careful private-state and mutation-isolation tests.

**Neutral**
- All evidence serializes through the canonical codec (ADR-01) and feeds the evidence bundle (ADR-05).

## Alternatives considered

- **A single aggregate SBOM keyed on the recipe.** Rejected — FR-15 requires per-built-artifact SBOMs; it blocks
  artifact and inventory consumers.
- **Link a scanner library.** Rejected — staying Apache-clean requires shelling the scanner.
- **Maintain a released-recipe index to map advisories to the fleet.** Rejected — it crosses the
  produce-vs-operate boundary.

## De-risking spikes

1. **Vuln-report determinism** — two runs over a fixture SBOM plus a pinned DB snapshot are byte-identical,
   offline.

## Follow-ups

- **ADR-05:** the SBOM and provenance digests are the per-artifact evidence rows the bundle aggregates.
- **ADR-09:** provenance is the companion evidence per built artifact.
- **ADR-16:** derives the exact required receipt types from Inventory plus promotion policy, and rejects
  `evidence: none` unless an exact, unexpired waiver receipt is authorized.
- **ADR-22 §7 / ADR-10:** the aggregate SBOM's dependency graph points from an aggregate to its members when
  they come from a curated pin set or from a `wrapped-build`'s `embeds` (aggregate → members), rather than
  presenting them as unrelated peers; the CycloneDX representation of that edge is this ADR's to fix.

## Acceptance evidence

Current module evidence in `pkg/nkd/sbom/*_test.go`, `pkg/nkd/vuln/*_test.go`,
`pkg/nkd/release/evidence_test.go`, and `pkg/nkd/catalog/prebuilt_test.go` covers deterministic documents,
prebuilt declarations, publisher-SBOM and publisher-evidence equality across online/mirror transport relocation,
opaque same-process qualification, exact HTTPS-reference byte verification, policy-driven consumption, waiver
replay, and mutation negatives. It does not prove signed cross-process receipt publication.

Acceptance additionally requires a boundary fixture that promotes same-process capabilities into canonical
signed receipts, publishes and reloads them, recomputes the exact artifact/evidence bytes, rejects receipt-type
and signer-role confusion, rejects replay to another artifact or policy, proves `evidence: none` fails an
evidence-required promotion, and proves only an exact unexpired waiver signed by the configured waiver authority
permits the documented exception.
